Chapter 1: Cell Biology

What is a Cell?

A cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life. All living organisms are made up of one or more cells.

Types of Cells

Cells can broadly be divided into two major types:

  • Prokaryotic cells: They do not have a true membrane-bound nucleus.
  • Eukaryotic cells: They contain a membrane-bound nucleus.

Important Cell Organelles

  • Nucleus: Contains genetic material and controls many cell activities.
  • Mitochondria: Produce ATP through cellular respiration.
  • Ribosomes: Responsible for protein synthesis.
  • Endoplasmic Reticulum: Helps in synthesis and transport of cellular materials.
  • Golgi Apparatus: Modifies, sorts and packages certain cell products.
  • Lysosomes: Contain digestive enzymes that help break down cellular materials.
  • Chloroplasts: Found in plants and are involved in photosynthesis.

📝 Practice MCQs

1. What is the basic structural and functional unit of life?

A) Tissue

B) Organ

C) Cell

D) Organ system

Answer: C) Cell

2. Which organelle is mainly responsible for protein synthesis?

A) Ribosome

B) Lysosome

C) Golgi apparatus

D) Vacuole

Answer: A) Ribosome

3. Which organelle is commonly known as the site of ATP production?

A) Nucleus

B) Mitochondrion

C) Ribosome

D) Golgi apparatus

Answer: B) Mitochondrion

4. Which type of cell lacks a membrane-bound nucleus?

A) Eukaryotic cell

B) Prokaryotic cell

C) Plant cell only

D) Animal cell only

Answer: B) Prokaryotic cell

5. Which organelle is involved in photosynthesis?

A) Chloroplast

B) Lysosome

C) Ribosome

D) Nucleus

Answer: A) Chloroplast

🎯 Quick Revision

  • Cell = basic unit of life.
  • Prokaryotic = no membrane-bound nucleus.
  • Eukaryotic = membrane-bound nucleus.
  • Ribosome = protein synthesis.
  • Mitochondria = major site of ATP production.
  • Chloroplast = photosynthesis in plants.

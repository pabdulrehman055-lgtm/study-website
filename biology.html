Chapter 1: Cell Biology

📚 What is a Cell?

A cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life. All living organisms are made up of one or more cells.

Important: The cell is considered the basic unit of life.

🔬 Types of Cells

1. Prokaryotic Cells

Prokaryotic cells are generally simple cells that do not have a membrane-bound nucleus.

  • They do not have a true nucleus.
  • Their genetic material is present in the nucleoid region.
  • Bacteria are examples of prokaryotic organisms.

2. Eukaryotic Cells

Eukaryotic cells have a true nucleus surrounded by a nuclear membrane. They also contain membrane-bound organelles.

  • Have a true nucleus.
  • Contain membrane-bound organelles.
  • Animals and plants have eukaryotic cells.

🧩 Important Cell Organelles

1. Nucleus

The nucleus contains genetic material and controls many activities of the cell.

2. Mitochondria

Mitochondria are involved in cellular respiration and production of ATP, which is an important energy currency of the cell.

3. Ribosomes

Ribosomes are the sites of protein synthesis.

4. Endoplasmic Reticulum

The endoplasmic reticulum helps in the synthesis and transport of various substances within the cell.

5. Golgi Apparatus

The Golgi apparatus modifies, sorts and packages certain cellular products.

6. Lysosomes

Lysosomes contain digestive enzymes and can break down unwanted materials inside cells.

7. Chloroplasts

Chloroplasts are found in plant cells and are the main sites of photosynthesis.

🌱 Plant Cell vs Animal Cell

Plant cells:

  • Have a cell wall.
  • Usually contain chloroplasts.
  • Usually have a large central vacuole.

Animal cells:

  • Do not have a cell wall.
  • Do not contain chloroplasts.
  • Usually have smaller vacuoles.

⚡ Quick Revision

  • Cell = basic unit of life.
  • Prokaryotes = no true nucleus.
  • Eukaryotes = true nucleus.
  • Mitochondria = cellular respiration and ATP production.
  • Ribosomes = protein synthesis.
  • Nucleus = contains genetic material.
  • Chloroplast = photosynthesis.
  • Plant cells have a cell wall.

📝 Practice MCQs

1. What is the basic unit of life?

A. Tissue
B. Cell
C. Organ
D. Atom

Answer: B. Cell
2. Which organelle is mainly associated with ATP production?

A. Ribosome
B. Nucleus
C. Mitochondria
D. Golgi apparatus

Answer: C. Mitochondria
3. Which cells do not have a true nucleus?

A. Eukaryotic cells
B. Prokaryotic cells
C. Plant cells only
D. Animal cells only

Answer: B. Prokaryotic cells
4. Where does protein synthesis occur?

A. Ribosomes
B. Lysosomes
C. Vacuoles
D. Chloroplasts

Answer: A. Ribosomes
5. Which organelle is responsible for photosynthesis?

A. Nucleus
B. Chloroplast
C. Mitochondria
D. Ribosome

Answer: B. Chloroplast

🎯 MDCAT Tip

For MDCAT preparation, remember the functions of important organelles and the major differences between prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells. These concepts are useful for solving basic Biology MCQs.

Chapter 5: Circular Motion & Rotational Motion

Circular Motion

Circular motion is the motion of an object along a circular path around a fixed point or axis.

Examples

  • Motion of a satellite around a planet
  • Motion of a fan blade
  • Motion of a stone tied to a string
  • Motion of a car around a circular track

Angular Displacement

Angular displacement is the angle through which an object rotates around a fixed axis.

Angular Velocity

Angular velocity is the rate of change of angular displacement with time.

Angular Velocity = Angular Displacement ÷ Time

Centripetal Force

The force required to keep an object moving in a circular path is called centripetal force. It is directed toward the centre of the circle.

F = mv²/r

Where m is mass, v is velocity and r is the radius of the circular path.

Important Point

Without a force directed toward the centre, an object would not continue in a circular path.

Rotational Motion

Rotational motion occurs when an object rotates about a fixed axis.

Examples

  • Rotation of a wheel
  • Rotation of the Earth around its axis
  • Rotation of a ceiling fan

Torque

Torque is the turning effect of a force about an axis.

Torque = Force × Perpendicular Distance

τ = F × r

Moment of Inertia

Moment of inertia is a measure of an object's resistance to changes in its rotational motion.

It depends on the mass of the object and how its mass is distributed relative to the axis of rotation.

SI Unit

The SI unit of moment of inertia is kg·m².

📝 Practice MCQs

1. In circular motion, centripetal force is directed toward the:

A) Outside of the circle

B) Centre of the circle

C) Top of the circle

D) Bottom of the circle

Answer: B) Centre of the circle

2. Which formula represents centripetal force?

A) F = ma

B) F = mv²/r

C) P = W/t

D) W = Fd

Answer: B) F = mv²/r

3. What is the turning effect of a force called?

A) Momentum

B) Energy

C) Torque

D) Power

Answer: C) Torque

4. Which of the following is an example of rotational motion?

A) A stationary book

B) A rotating wheel

C) A straight road

D) A parked car

Answer: B) A rotating wheel

5. What is the SI unit of moment of inertia?

A) kg·m

B) kg·m²

C) N/m

D) J/s

Answer: B) kg·m²

🎯 Quick Revision

  • Circular motion → motion along a circular path
  • Centripetal force → directed toward the centre
  • Centripetal force → F = mv²/r
  • Rotational motion → rotation about an axis
  • Torque → turning effect of force
  • Torque → τ = F × r
  • Moment of inertia unit → kg·m²

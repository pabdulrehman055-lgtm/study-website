📖 Class 9 English Notes

📚 Grammar

Parts of Speech

Noun, Pronoun, Verb, Adjective, Adverb, Preposition, Conjunction and Interjection.

Present Simple

Used for habits, routines and general facts.

Example: I study English every day.

Past Simple

Used for completed actions in the past.

Example: I studied English yesterday.

Future Simple

Used for actions that will happen in the future.

Example: I will study English tomorrow.

🧠 Vocabulary

Important = Very necessary
Improve = Make better
Knowledge = Information and understanding
Practice = Repeated activity to improve a skill

4. Write one sentence in the future simple tense.
5. What is the meaning of "Improve"?

📘 Chapter 1: Grammar

Parts of Speech

The main parts of speech are noun, pronoun, verb, adjective, adverb, preposition, conjunction and interjection.

Subject and Verb

The verb should agree with the subject. Example: She plays cricket.

Active and Passive Voice

Active voice focuses on the person doing the action. Passive voice focuses on the action or receiver.

📗 Chapter 2: Tenses

Present Simple

Example: He goes to school every day.

Past Simple

Example: He went to school yesterday.

Future Simple

Example: He will go to school tomorrow.

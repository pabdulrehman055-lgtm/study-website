1. Modern Physics

Modern physics deals with physical phenomena that cannot be completely explained by classical physics.

Important topics include quantum theory, photons, the photoelectric effect, relativity and matter waves.

2. Quantum Theory

According to quantum theory, energy is exchanged in discrete packets called quanta.

For electromagnetic radiation, these energy packets are called photons.

E = hf

Here, E is photon energy, h is Planck's constant and f is frequency.

3. Photon

A photon is a quantum or packet of electromagnetic radiation.

The energy of a photon depends directly on its frequency.

E = hf

Higher frequency means higher photon energy.

4. Photoelectric Effect

The photoelectric effect is the emission of electrons from a metal surface when electromagnetic radiation of suitable frequency falls on it.

The emitted electrons are called photoelectrons.

The minimum frequency required to eject electrons from a metal is called the threshold frequency.

5. Einstein's Photoelectric Equation

Einstein explained the photoelectric effect using the photon concept.

hf = φ + K.Emax

Here, φ is the work function of the metal and K.Emax is the maximum kinetic energy of the emitted electron.

6. Relativity

Einstein's theory of relativity describes the relationship between space, time, matter and energy.

A famous relation between mass and energy is:

E = mc²

Here, m is mass and c is the speed of light in vacuum.

7. Matter Waves

According to de Broglie's hypothesis, moving particles can exhibit wave-like properties.

The wavelength associated with a moving particle is called the de Broglie wavelength.

λ = h / p

Here, λ is wavelength, h is Planck's constant and p is momentum.

8. Atomic Energy Levels

Electrons in an atom can occupy specific energy levels.

When an electron moves from a higher energy level to a lower energy level, energy can be emitted as electromagnetic radiation.

When an electron absorbs suitable energy, it can move to a higher energy level.

📝 Practice MCQs

1. The energy of a photon is given by:

A) E = mc

B) E = hf

C) E = mv

D) E = ma

Answer: B) E = hf

2. The minimum frequency required for photoelectric emission is called:

A) Resonant frequency

B) Natural frequency

C) Threshold frequency

D) Angular frequency

Answer: C) Threshold frequency

3. Einstein's mass-energy relation is:

A) E = hf

B) E = mc²

C) F = ma

D) P = VI

Answer: B) E = mc²

4. According to de Broglie, moving particles have:

A) Only electric properties

B) Only magnetic properties

C) Wave-like properties

D) No physical properties

Answer: C) Wave-like properties

5. A photon is a packet of:

A) Matter

B) Electromagnetic radiation

C) Sound

D) Mechanical energy

Answer: B) Electromagnetic radiation

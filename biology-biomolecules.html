📚 What are Biomolecules?

Biomolecules are chemical substances found in living organisms. They are important for building cells, producing energy, storing information and carrying out biological processes.

Major biomolecules: Carbohydrates, lipids, proteins and nucleic acids.

🍞 1. Carbohydrates

Carbohydrates are organic compounds mainly composed of carbon, hydrogen and oxygen. They are an important source of energy for living organisms.

Types of Carbohydrates

  • Monosaccharides: Simple sugars such as glucose and fructose.
  • Disaccharides: Sugars made from two monosaccharides, such as sucrose.
  • Polysaccharides: Large carbohydrates such as starch, glycogen and cellulose.
Important: Glucose is an important respiratory substrate and source of energy.

🥑 2. Lipids

Lipids are a group of water-insoluble organic compounds. They include fats, oils, phospholipids and steroids.

Functions of Lipids

  • Store energy.
  • Help form cell membranes.
  • Provide insulation.
  • Some lipids are involved in signalling and other biological functions.
Remember: Phospholipids are important components of biological membranes.

🥩 3. Proteins

Proteins are large biological molecules made from amino acids. They perform many important functions in living organisms.

Functions of Proteins

  • Enzymes are mostly proteins.
  • Some proteins provide structural support.
  • Some proteins transport substances.
  • Some proteins are involved in defence.
  • Some proteins act as hormones or receptors.

Amino Acids

Amino acids are the building blocks of proteins. Amino acids join together through peptide bonds to form polypeptide chains.

🧬 4. Nucleic Acids

Nucleic acids are biomolecules involved in storing and transmitting genetic information.

DNA

DNA stands for deoxyribonucleic acid. It stores genetic information in cells.

RNA

RNA stands for ribonucleic acid. Different types of RNA have important roles in gene expression and protein synthesis.

Important: DNA and RNA are nucleic acids made from nucleotide units.

⚡ Quick Revision

  • Biomolecules are important chemical substances of living organisms.
  • Carbohydrates are an important source of energy.
  • Glucose is a monosaccharide.
  • Starch and glycogen are polysaccharides.
  • Lipids include fats, oils and phospholipids.
  • Amino acids are the building blocks of proteins.
  • Proteins perform many structural and functional roles.
  • DNA and RNA are nucleic acids.
  • Nucleotides are the basic units of nucleic acids.

📝 MDCAT Practice MCQs

1. Which of the following is a monosaccharide?

A. Starch
B. Glycogen
C. Glucose
D. Cellulose

Answer: C. Glucose
2. What are proteins made from?

A. Fatty acids only
B. Amino acids
C. Glucose molecules
D. Nucleotides

Answer: B. Amino acids
3. Which biomolecule is an important component of cell membranes?

A. Phospholipid
B. Glucose
C. DNA
D. Starch

Answer: A. Phospholipid
4. Which molecules store genetic information?

A. Lipids
B. Carbohydrates
C. Nucleic acids
D. Minerals

Answer: C. Nucleic acids
5. What is the basic unit of nucleic acids?

A. Amino acid
B. Nucleotide
C. Fatty acid
D. Glucose

Answer: B. Nucleotide

🎯 MDCAT Tip

Memorize the building blocks and major functions of carbohydrates, lipids, proteins and nucleic acids. These relationships are useful when solving Biology MCQs.

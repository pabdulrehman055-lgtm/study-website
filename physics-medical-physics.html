1. Medical Physics

Medical physics is the application of physics principles and techniques in medicine and healthcare.

It is used in medical diagnosis, medical imaging, radiation treatment and monitoring of patients.

2. X-Rays

X-rays are high-energy electromagnetic waves. They can pass through soft tissues and are absorbed more strongly by dense materials such as bones.

X-rays are commonly used to produce images of bones and other internal structures of the body.

3. Ultrasound

Ultrasound uses high-frequency sound waves to produce images of structures inside the body.

Ultrasound is commonly used during pregnancy and for examining organs and tissues.

Wave speed = Frequency × Wavelength

4. Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI)

MRI uses a strong magnetic field and radio waves to produce detailed images of structures inside the body.

MRI does not use ionizing X-ray radiation.

5. Computed Tomography (CT Scan)

A CT scan uses X-rays and computer processing to create detailed cross-sectional images of the body.

CT scans can provide more detailed images than a conventional X-ray in many situations.

6. Nuclear Medicine

Nuclear medicine uses small amounts of radioactive substances for diagnosis and treatment.

A radioactive substance may be introduced into the body and its radiation can be detected using special equipment.

7. Radiotherapy

Radiotherapy uses high-energy radiation to destroy or control cancer cells.

The radiation is carefully directed toward the treatment area to reduce damage to surrounding healthy tissues.

8. Radiation Safety

Ionizing radiation can damage living tissues, so radiation must be used carefully.

Important principles of radiation protection include:

  • Minimize exposure time.
  • Increase distance from the radiation source.
  • Use appropriate shielding.

9. Medical Applications of Physics

  • X-ray imaging
  • Ultrasound imaging
  • MRI
  • CT scanning
  • Nuclear medicine
  • Radiotherapy

📝 Practice MCQs

1. Which type of wave is used in ultrasound?

A) Radio waves

B) Sound waves

C) X-rays

D) Gamma rays

Answer: B) Sound waves

2. MRI mainly uses:

A) X-rays only

B) Magnetic fields and radio waves

C) Visible light

D) Sound waves only

Answer: B) Magnetic fields and radio waves

3. Which technique uses X-rays and computer processing?

A) MRI

B) Ultrasound

C) CT scan

D) ECG

Answer: C) CT scan

4. Radiotherapy is mainly used to:

A) Measure body temperature

B) Treat cancer

C) Measure blood pressure

D) Produce sound

Answer: B) Treat cancer

5. Which is an important radiation protection method?

A) Increasing exposure time

B) Reducing distance

C) Using shielding

D) Removing protection

Answer: C) Using shielding

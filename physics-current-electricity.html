⚡ 1. What is Electric Current?

Electric current is the rate of flow of electric charge through a conductor.

I = Q / t

Where I is current, Q is charge and t is time.

The SI unit of electric current is the ampere (A).

🔋 2. Potential Difference

Potential difference is the work done per unit charge in moving a charge between two points.

V = W / Q

The SI unit of potential difference is the volt (V).

🧱 3. Resistance

Resistance is the opposition offered by a conductor to the flow of electric current.

The SI unit of resistance is the ohm (Ω).

R = V / I

Resistance depends on the nature, length and cross-sectional area of a conductor.

📐 4. Ohm's Law

Ohm's law states that, at constant temperature, the current through a conductor is directly proportional to the potential difference across it.

V = IR

Where V is voltage, I is current and R is resistance.

🔌 5. Series Combination of Resistors

When resistors are connected one after another in a single path, they are said to be connected in series.

R = R₁ + R₂ + R₃ + ...

In a series circuit, the same current flows through each resistor.

🔌 6. Parallel Combination of Resistors

When resistors are connected across the same two points, they are connected in parallel.

1/R = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + 1/R₃ + ...

In a parallel circuit, the potential difference across each branch is the same.

💡 7. Electric Power

Electric power is the rate at which electrical energy is converted or used.

P = VI

The SI unit of electric power is the watt (W).

🔋 8. Electrical Energy

Electrical energy is the energy transferred when electric current flows through a circuit.

E = Pt

Electrical energy is commonly measured in joules (J).

📝 Practice MCQs

1. The SI unit of electric current is:

A) Volt

B) Ohm

C) Ampere

D) Watt

Answer: C) Ampere

2. Which formula represents Ohm's law?

A) F = ma

B) V = IR

C) P = W/t

D) Q = mcΔT

Answer: B) V = IR

3. The SI unit of resistance is:

A) Ampere

B) Volt

C) Ohm

D) Watt

Answer: C) Ohm

4. In a series circuit, the same quantity through each resistor is:

A) Voltage

B) Current

C) Resistance

D) Power

Answer: B) Current

5. The formula for electric power is:

A) P = VI

B) P = V/I

C) P = I/V

D) P = Q/t

Answer: A) P = VI

⚡ Quick Revision

  • Electric current is the rate of flow of charge.
  • Current: I = Q/t.
  • Potential difference: V = W/Q.
  • Resistance opposes the flow of current.
  • Ohm's law: V = IR.
  • Series resistance: R = R₁ + R₂ + R₃.
  • Electric power: P = VI.
  • Electrical energy: E = Pt.

⚡ Unit 2: Force and Motion

MDCAT Physics - Complete Notes & 15 Practice MCQs

1. Motion

Motion is the change in position of an object with respect to a reference point as time passes.

An object is said to be at rest when its position does not change with respect to the reference point.

2. Distance

Distance is the total path length travelled by an object. It is a scalar quantity.

The SI unit of distance is metre (m).

3. Displacement

Displacement is the shortest straight-line distance from the initial position to the final position, together with its direction.

Displacement is a vector quantity.

Displacement = Final Position − Initial Position

4. Speed

Speed is the rate of change of distance with respect to time.

Speed = Distance ÷ Time

SI unit of speed is metre per second (m/s).

5. Velocity

Velocity is the rate of change of displacement with respect to time.

Velocity = Displacement ÷ Time

Velocity is a vector quantity because it has magnitude and direction.

6. Acceleration

Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity with respect to time.

a = (v - u) ÷ t

Where:

  • u = initial velocity
  • v = final velocity
  • t = time
  • a = acceleration

SI unit of acceleration is m/s².

7. Equations of Motion

For uniformly accelerated motion, the following equations are important:

v = u + at

s = ut + ½at²

v² = u² + 2as

These equations are commonly used to solve numerical problems involving motion.

8. Force

Force is a push or pull that can change the state of motion of an object.

Force is a vector quantity.

F = ma

The SI unit of force is newton (N).

9. Newton's First Law

Newton's first law states that an object remains at rest or continues moving with uniform velocity in a straight line unless acted upon by an external unbalanced force.

This law is also called the law of inertia.

10. Newton's Second Law

Newton's second law relates force, mass and acceleration.

F = ma

For a constant mass, greater force produces greater acceleration.

11. Newton's Third Law

Newton's third law states that for every action there is an equal and opposite reaction.

Action and reaction forces act on different objects.

12. Momentum

Momentum is the product of mass and velocity.

p = mv

Momentum is a vector quantity.

SI unit of momentum is kg·m/s.

13. Impulse

Impulse is equal to the change in momentum of an object.

Impulse = F × Δt

Impulse = Δp

14. Friction

Friction is a force that opposes the relative motion or tendency of motion between surfaces in contact.

Friction can be useful, for example, it allows vehicles to move and helps us walk.

15. Important MDCAT Points

  • Distance is scalar.
  • Displacement is vector.
  • Speed is scalar.
  • Velocity is vector.
  • Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity.
  • Force = mass × acceleration.
  • Momentum = mass × velocity.
  • Newton's first law is the law of inertia.
  • Newton's third law involves equal and opposite forces.
  • Friction opposes relative motion.

📝 MDCAT Practice MCQs

1. Which of the following is a scalar quantity?

A) Velocity

B) Displacement

C) Speed

D) Acceleration

Answer: C) Speed

2. Which quantity has both magnitude and direction?

A) Distance

B) Speed

C) Velocity

D) Time

Answer: C) Velocity

3. The SI unit of acceleration is:

A) m/s

B) m/s²

C) kg/m

D) N/m

Answer: B) m/s²

4. Speed is equal to:

A) Time ÷ Distance

B) Distance × Time

C) Distance ÷ Time

D) Distance + Time

Answer: C) Distance ÷ Time

5. The formula for acceleration is:

A) a = v + u

B) a = (v - u)/t

C) a = vt

D) a = u/v

Answer: B) a = (v - u)/t

6. Newton's first law is also known as the law of:

A) Momentum

B) Inertia

C) Gravitation

D) Acceleration

Answer: B) Inertia

7. Newton's second law is represented by:

A) F = ma

B) p = mv

C) v = u + at

D) W = Fd

Answer: A) F = ma

8. The SI unit of force is:

A) Joule

B) Watt

C) Newton

D) Pascal

Answer: C) Newton

9. Momentum is given by:

A) p = ma

B) p = mv

C) p = m/v

D) p = v/m

Answer: B) p = mv

10. Momentum is a:

A) Scalar quantity

B) Vector quantity

C) Unitless quantity

D) Constant quantity

Answer: B) Vector quantity

11. Newton's third law states that:

A) F = ma

B) Every action has an equal and opposite reaction

C) Objects remain at rest forever

D) Momentum is always zero

Answer: B) Every action has an equal and opposite reaction

12. Which force opposes relative motion between surfaces?

A) Gravitational force

B) Magnetic force

C) Friction

D) Electric force

Answer: C) Friction

13. Impulse is equal to:

A) Change in momentum

B) Change in mass

C) Change in distance

D) Change in temperature

Answer: A) Change in momentum

14. If the net force on an object is zero, its acceleration is:

A) Maximum

B) Zero

C) Infinite

D) Negative always

Answer: B) Zero

15. Which equation is used for uniformly accelerated motion?

A) v = u + at

B) F = ma only

C) p = mv only

D) P = W/t only

Answer: A) v = u + at

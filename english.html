Grammar

Parts of Speech

Noun, Pronoun, Verb, Adjective, Adverb, Preposition, Conjunction and Interjection.

Present Simple

We use the present simple for habits and general facts.

Example: I study English every day.

Past Simple

We use the past simple for completed actions in the past.

Example: I studied English yesterday.

Vocabulary

Important = Very necessary

Improve = Make better

Learn = Gain knowledge

Practice = Do something repeatedly to improve

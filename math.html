📐 Mathematics Notes

📚 Important Formulas

Area of Rectangle: Length × Width
Area of Square: Side × Side
Area of Triangle: ½ × Base × Height
Speed: Distance ÷ Time
Percentage: (Part ÷ Total) × 100

✏️ Practice Questions

1. What is 25 × 4?
2. Find the area of a rectangle with length 10 cm and width 5 cm.
3. A car travels 120 km in 2 hours. What is its speed?
4. Find 20% of 200.
5. Find the area of a square with side 6 cm.

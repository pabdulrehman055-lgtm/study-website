Chapter Overview

Biotechnology is the use of biological organisms, cells or biological processes to produce useful products and solve practical problems.

MDCAT Focus: Understand recombinant DNA technology, restriction enzymes, DNA ligase, vectors, plasmids, PCR, genetic engineering and major applications of biotechnology.

1. What is Biotechnology?

Biotechnology combines biology with technology to develop useful products and processes.

Examples include:

  • Production of medicines
  • Production of vaccines
  • Improvement of crops
  • Industrial production of enzymes
  • Genetic testing
  • Production of recombinant proteins

2. Genetic Engineering

Genetic engineering involves deliberately changing or manipulating an organism's DNA.

A gene from one organism can be isolated and inserted into another organism using molecular techniques.

Key Idea: Genetic engineering allows scientists to modify genetic material for a specific purpose.

3. Recombinant DNA

Recombinant DNA is DNA formed by joining DNA fragments from different sources.

A gene of interest can be inserted into a suitable DNA vector. The recombinant DNA can then be introduced into a host cell.

Exam Tip: Recombinant DNA technology is a major tool of genetic engineering.

4. Restriction Enzymes

Restriction enzymes are enzymes that recognize specific DNA sequences and cut DNA at or near those sites.

They are important in recombinant DNA technology because they allow scientists to cut DNA into specific fragments.

Remember: Restriction enzyme → cuts DNA at specific recognition sequences.

5. DNA Ligase

DNA ligase is an enzyme that joins DNA fragments by forming phosphodiester bonds in the DNA backbone.

Easy Memory Trick:
Restriction enzyme = cuts DNA
DNA ligase = joins DNA

6. Plasmids

Plasmids are small, usually circular DNA molecules found in bacteria. They can replicate independently of the bacterial chromosome.

Plasmids are commonly used as vectors in genetic engineering.

  • Small DNA molecules
  • Usually circular
  • Commonly found in bacteria
  • Can replicate independently
  • Can carry foreign DNA

7. Vectors

A vector is a DNA molecule used to carry foreign genetic material into a host cell.

Common Examples

  • Plasmids
  • Some bacteriophages
  • Other engineered DNA vectors
MDCAT Tip: Plasmids are important biological vectors used to introduce recombinant DNA into bacterial cells.

8. Basic Steps of Recombinant DNA Technology

  1. Identify the gene of interest.
  2. Isolate the required DNA.
  3. Cut DNA using suitable restriction enzymes.
  4. Insert the desired DNA fragment into a vector.
  5. Join the DNA using DNA ligase.
  6. Introduce recombinant DNA into a host cell.
  7. Select cells carrying the desired recombinant DNA.
  8. Allow the host cells to multiply or produce the desired product.
Simple Sequence:
Cut → Insert → Join → Transfer → Select → Multiply

9. PCR — Polymerase Chain Reaction

PCR is a laboratory technique used to make many copies of a specific DNA sequence.

It is useful when only a small amount of DNA is initially available.

Basic PCR Cycle

  • Denaturation: DNA strands separate.
  • Annealing: Primers bind to complementary DNA sequences.
  • Extension: DNA polymerase synthesizes new DNA strands.
High-Yield Point: PCR is used for amplification of a specific DNA sequence.

10. DNA Polymerase in PCR

DNA polymerase is the enzyme responsible for synthesizing new DNA strands during the extension stage of PCR.

A heat-stable DNA polymerase is used so that the enzyme can function through repeated heating cycles.

11. Applications in Medicine

Biotechnology has many applications in medicine.

  • Production of recombinant therapeutic proteins
  • Development of vaccines
  • Genetic testing
  • Diagnosis of some diseases
  • Production of certain hormones and enzymes
  • Research into genetic disorders
Example: Recombinant DNA technology can be used to produce human insulin using genetically modified microorganisms.

12. Biotechnology in Agriculture

Biotechnology can be used to improve agricultural crops.

  • Improved resistance to pests
  • Improved resistance to certain diseases
  • Improved crop characteristics
  • Development of crops with useful traits

Genetically modified organisms may contain introduced genetic material that provides a desired characteristic.

13. Biotechnology in Industry

Microorganisms and enzymes are widely used in biotechnology and industrial processes.

  • Food production
  • Fermentation
  • Enzyme production
  • Production of useful chemicals
  • Waste treatment

14. Gene Therapy — Basic Concept

Gene therapy is an approach in which genetic material is introduced into cells to help treat or manage certain genetic conditions.

The aim may be to replace, repair or compensate for a faulty genetic function.

Remember: Gene therapy focuses on using genetic material as part of a treatment strategy.

15. Cloning

Cloning refers to producing genetically very similar or identical copies of a biological entity.

Cloning can refer to genes, cells or organisms depending on the context.

Important: Cloning and genetic engineering are related concepts but are not exactly the same process.

16. Biosafety and Ethical Considerations

Biotechnology provides many benefits, but its applications also require careful scientific, environmental and ethical consideration.

  • Safe handling of genetically modified organisms
  • Responsible use of genetic information
  • Environmental considerations
  • Ethical issues related to genetic technologies

17. Quick Revision

  • Biotechnology uses biological systems for useful purposes.
  • Genetic engineering modifies genetic material.
  • Recombinant DNA contains DNA joined from different sources.
  • Restriction enzymes cut DNA at specific sequences.
  • DNA ligase joins DNA fragments.
  • Plasmids are commonly used as vectors.
  • Vectors carry foreign DNA into host cells.
  • PCR amplifies a specific DNA sequence.
  • PCR includes denaturation, annealing and extension.
  • DNA polymerase synthesizes new DNA during PCR extension.
  • Biotechnology is used in medicine, agriculture and industry.
  • Gene therapy uses genetic material as part of treatment strategies.

18. MDCAT-Style MCQs

1. Which enzyme cuts DNA at specific recognition sequences?

A) DNA ligase

B) Restriction enzyme

C) Amylase

D) Lipase

Answer: B) Restriction enzyme

2. Which enzyme joins DNA fragments?

A) DNA ligase

B) Restriction enzyme

C) Helicase

D) Amylase

Answer: A) DNA ligase

3. A common vector used in genetic engineering is:

A) Ribosome

B) Plasmid

C) Lysosome

D) Golgi body

Answer: B) Plasmid

4. PCR is mainly used to:

A) Destroy DNA

B) Amplify a specific DNA sequence

C) Digest proteins

D) Produce ATP

Answer: B) Amplify a specific DNA sequence

5. During PCR, DNA strands separate during:

A) Annealing

B) Extension

C) Denaturation

D) Translation

Answer: C) Denaturation

6. During PCR, primers bind to DNA during:

A) Denaturation

B) Annealing

C) Extension

D) Replication termination

Answer: B) Annealing

7. DNA polymerase is mainly involved in:

A) DNA synthesis

B) Protein digestion

C) Lipid storage

D) RNA destruction

Answer: A) DNA synthesis

8. Recombinant DNA is formed by:

A) Destroying all DNA

B) Joining DNA fragments from different sources

C) Removing all genes

D) Converting DNA into protein

Answer: B) Joining DNA fragments from different sources

9. Plasmids are commonly found in:

A) Bacteria

B) Red blood cells only

C) Bone tissue only

D) Viruses only

Answer: A) Bacteria

10. Which technology can be used to produce recombinant human insulin?

A) Recombinant DNA technology

B) Photosynthesis

C) Osmosis

D) Transpiration

Answer: A) Recombinant DNA technology

11. The main purpose of a vector is to:

A) Carry foreign DNA into a host cell

B) Digest carbohydrates

C) Produce oxygen

D) Destroy proteins

Answer: A) Carry foreign DNA into a host cell

12. Which is NOT a basic stage of PCR?

A) Denaturation

B) Annealing

C) Extension

D) Translation

Answer: D) Translation

13. Genetic engineering involves:

A) Deliberate manipulation of genetic material

B) Digestion of food only

C) Production of sweat

D) Movement of bones

Answer: A) Deliberate manipulation of genetic material

14. Which field can benefit from biotechnology?

A) Medicine

B) Agriculture

C) Industry

D) All of these

Answer: D) All of these

15. Which enzyme is used to join DNA fragments?

A) Ligase

B) Pepsin

C) Trypsin

D) Amylase

Answer: A) Ligase

19. MDCAT Exam Tips

  • Restriction enzyme → cuts DNA.
  • DNA ligase → joins DNA.
  • Plasmid → common vector.
  • PCR → DNA amplification.
  • Denaturation → DNA strands separate.
  • Annealing → primers bind.
  • Extension → new DNA is synthesized.
  • DNA polymerase → synthesizes DNA.
High-Yield MCQ: If the question asks which enzyme joins DNA fragments, the answer is DNA LIGASE.

🧠 Logical Reasoning

Logical Reasoning means using information, rules and relationships to reach a correct conclusion.

This StudyHub page provides basic reasoning concepts and MDCAT-style practice questions.

Practice Tip: Logical Reasoning improves mainly through regular practice. Try to solve questions without looking at the answer first.

1. Statements and Conclusions

A statement gives information. A conclusion is an idea that logically follows from the given information.

Example:
Statement: All doctors are educated.
Ali is a doctor.
Conclusion: Ali is educated.

2. Analogy

An analogy compares two pairs that have a similar relationship.

Example:
Doctor : Hospital :: Teacher : School

The relationship is: Person : Workplace

3. Number Series

In a number series, numbers follow a particular pattern. Your task is to identify the pattern and find the next number.

Example:
2, 4, 6, 8, 10, ?

Each number increases by 2.
Answer = 12

Common Patterns

  • Addition
  • Subtraction
  • Multiplication
  • Division
  • Alternating patterns
  • Square numbers

4. Letter Series

Letter series questions use alphabetical positions or patterns.

Example:
A, C, E, G, ?

Every second letter is selected.
Answer = I

5. Coding and Decoding

In coding-decoding questions, a word or number is changed according to a specific rule.

Example:
If CAT becomes DBU, each letter has moved one position forward in the alphabet.

C → D
A → B
T → U

6. Classification

Classification questions ask you to find the item that does not belong to the same group.

Example:
Apple, Mango, Banana, Carrot

Carrot is different because the others are commonly classified as fruits.

7. Direction Sense

Direction questions test your ability to track movement and determine the final direction or position.

Basic Directions

  • North
  • South
  • East
  • West
Draw a small diagram when a direction question becomes confusing. It can make the answer much easier.

8. Blood Relations

Blood-relation questions require you to identify relationships between people.

Example:
Ahmed is the brother of Sara. Sara is the daughter of Ali. Therefore Ahmed is also the son of Ali.

For difficult questions, draw a simple family tree.

9. Odd One Out

In an odd-one-out question, identify the option that follows a different rule from the others.

Example:
2, 4, 6, 9

9 is the odd one because the other numbers are even.

10. Logical Sequence

Some questions provide events or steps in a mixed order. You must arrange them logically.

Example:
Seed → Plant → Flower → Fruit

⚡ Quick Revision

  • Read the question carefully.
  • Identify the relationship or pattern.
  • Do not assume information that is not given.
  • For number series, check differences first.
  • For letter series, check alphabet positions.
  • For directions, draw a simple diagram.
  • For blood relations, draw a family tree.
  • For analogies, identify the relationship between the first pair.
  • For coding-decoding, find the exact transformation rule.

📝 MDCAT-Style Practice MCQs

1. Find the next number: 2, 4, 6, 8, ?

A) 9
B) 10
C) 11
D) 12

Answer: B) 10

2. Find the next letter: A, C, E, G, ?

A) H
B) I
C) J
D) K

Answer: B) I

3. Doctor : Hospital :: Teacher : ?

A) Market
B) School
C) Bank
D) Farm

Answer: B) School

4. Which is the odd one out?

A) Apple
B) Mango
C) Banana
D) Carrot

Answer: D) Carrot

5. If CAT becomes DBU, then DOG becomes:

A) EPH
B) FPH
C) EOG
D) DPH

Answer: A) EPH

6. A person walks north and then turns right. Which direction is he facing?

A) West
B) South
C) East
D) North

Answer: C) East

7. Find the next number: 5, 10, 15, 20, ?

A) 22
B) 23
C) 24
D) 25

Answer: D) 25

8. Which number is different?

A) 2
B) 4
C) 6
D) 9

Answer: D) 9

9. If all doctors are educated and Ali is a doctor, what follows?

A) Ali is educated
B) Ali is a teacher
C) Ali is a scientist
D) Nothing follows

Answer: A) Ali is educated

10. Find the next number: 3, 6, 12, 24, ?

A) 36
B) 42
C) 48
D) 54

Answer: C) 48

11. Seed : Plant :: Egg : ?

A) Stone
B) Bird
C) Water
D) Soil

Answer: B) Bird

12. A person facing east turns left. Which direction is he facing?

A) North
B) South
C) West
D) East

Answer: A) North

13. Find the next number: 100, 90, 80, 70, ?

A) 50
B) 55
C) 60
D) 65

Answer: C) 60

14. If Ali is the brother of Sara and Sara is the daughter of Ahmed, Ali is Ahmed's:

A) Brother
B) Son
C) Father
D) Uncle

Answer: B) Son

15. Which comes next? Monday, Wednesday, Friday, ?

A) Saturday
B) Sunday
C) Tuesday
D) Thursday

Answer: B) Sunday

🎯 Exam Tips

  • Do not spend too much time on one difficult question.
  • Look for simple patterns first.
  • Use diagrams for directions and relationships.
  • Practice number and letter series regularly.
  • Always read every option before selecting an answer.
  • Practice under a time limit.

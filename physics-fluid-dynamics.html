Chapter 6: Fluid Dynamics

Fluids

A fluid is a substance that can flow and take the shape of its container. Liquids and gases are fluids.

Density

Density is the mass per unit volume of a substance.

Density = Mass ÷ Volume

ρ = m/V

SI Unit

The SI unit of density is kg/m³.

Pressure

Pressure is the force acting normally per unit area.

Pressure = Force ÷ Area

P = F/A

SI Unit

The SI unit of pressure is pascal (Pa).

Fluid Flow

The motion of a fluid from one place to another is called fluid flow.

Streamline Flow

In streamline flow, fluid particles move along smooth, well-defined paths.

Continuity Equation

For an incompressible fluid flowing through a pipe, the product of cross-sectional area and velocity remains constant.

A₁v₁ = A₂v₂

Bernoulli's Principle

Bernoulli's principle states that for steady flow of an ideal fluid, an increase in the speed of the fluid is associated with a decrease in its pressure.

Applications

  • Airplane wings
  • Sprayers
  • Atomizers
  • Flow of fluids through pipes

📝 Practice MCQs

1. Which of the following is a fluid?

A) Liquid

B) Gas

C) Both A and B

D) Solid only

Answer: C) Both A and B

2. Density is defined as:

A) Volume ÷ Mass

B) Mass ÷ Volume

C) Force ÷ Area

D) Mass × Volume

Answer: B) Mass ÷ Volume

3. What is the SI unit of pressure?

A) Newton

B) Joule

C) Pascal

D) Watt

Answer: C) Pascal

4. Which equation represents the continuity equation?

A) F = ma

B) P = F/A

C) A₁v₁ = A₂v₂

D) W = Fd

Answer: C) A₁v₁ = A₂v₂

5. According to Bernoulli's principle, when fluid speed increases, pressure generally:

A) Increases

B) Decreases

C) Becomes zero

D) Remains infinite

Answer: B) Decreases

🎯 Quick Revision

  • Fluid → substance that can flow
  • Density = Mass ÷ Volume
  • Density unit → kg/m³
  • Pressure = Force ÷ Area
  • Pressure unit → Pascal (Pa)
  • Continuity equation → A₁v₁ = A₂v₂
  • Bernoulli → higher fluid speed generally means lower pressure

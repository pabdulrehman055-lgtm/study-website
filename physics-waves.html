Chapter 7: Waves

Waves

A wave is a disturbance that transfers energy from one place to another without transferring matter as a whole.

Types of Waves

  • Mechanical waves
  • Electromagnetic waves

Mechanical Waves

Mechanical waves require a material medium for their propagation.

Examples include sound waves and waves on a rope.

Electromagnetic Waves

Electromagnetic waves can travel through vacuum and do not require a material medium.

Examples include visible light and radio waves.

Transverse and Longitudinal Waves

Transverse Waves

In a transverse wave, particles of the medium vibrate perpendicular to the direction of wave propagation.

Examples include waves on a stretched string.

Longitudinal Waves

In a longitudinal wave, particles of the medium vibrate parallel to the direction of wave propagation.

Sound waves in air are an example of longitudinal waves.

Important Wave Quantities

Wavelength

Wavelength is the distance between two successive points in the same phase, such as two consecutive crests or troughs.

It is represented by λ.

Frequency

Frequency is the number of complete vibrations or cycles per second.

The SI unit of frequency is hertz (Hz).

Time Period

Time period is the time required to complete one vibration.

T = 1/f

Wave Speed

The speed of a wave is related to its frequency and wavelength.

v = fλ

Amplitude

Amplitude is the maximum displacement of a vibrating particle from its mean position.

Greater amplitude generally means greater energy carried by the wave.

📝 Practice MCQs

1. A wave mainly transfers:

A) Matter

B) Energy

C) Mass only

D) Volume

Answer: B) Energy

2. Which type of wave requires a material medium?

A) Mechanical wave

B) Electromagnetic wave

C) Light wave

D) Radio wave

Answer: A) Mechanical wave

3. What is the SI unit of frequency?

A) Joule

B) Newton

C) Hertz

D) Watt

Answer: C) Hertz

4. Which formula represents wave speed?

A) v = fλ

B) F = ma

C) P = W/t

D) W = Fd

Answer: A) v = fλ

5. Sound waves in air are generally:

A) Transverse

B) Longitudinal

C) Electromagnetic

D) Stationary only

Answer: B) Longitudinal

🎯 Quick Revision

  • Wave → transfers energy from one place to another
  • Mechanical waves → require a medium
  • Electromagnetic waves → can travel through vacuum
  • Frequency unit → Hertz (Hz)
  • Time period → T = 1/f
  • Wave speed → v = fλ
  • Transverse → vibration perpendicular to propagation
  • Longitudinal → vibration parallel to propagation

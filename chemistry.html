1. Fundamental Concepts of Chemistry

🔹 Mole

A mole is the amount of substance containing 6.022 × 10²³ particles.

The particles may be atoms, molecules, ions or other microscopic particles.

Avogadro's Number = 6.022 × 10²³ particles/mol

🔹 Molar Mass

Molar mass is the mass of one mole of a substance. Its unit is g/mol.

Number of moles = Mass ÷ Molar Mass

n = m / M

🔹 Example

Molecular mass of H₂O:

H = 1 × 2 = 2
O = 16
Total = 18 g/mol

Therefore, 18 g of water = 1 mole of H₂O.

🔹 Stoichiometry

Stoichiometry deals with the quantitative relationship between reactants and products in a chemical reaction.

Moles → Mole Ratio → Required Quantity

🔹 Limiting Reactant

The reactant that is completely consumed first and limits the amount of product formed is called the limiting reactant.

🔹 Excess Reactant

A reactant that remains after the limiting reactant has been completely consumed is called the excess reactant.

🔹 Percentage Yield

Percentage Yield = (Actual Yield ÷ Theoretical Yield) × 100

2. Atomic Structure

An atom is the basic unit of an element.

Particle Charge Location
Proton +1 Nucleus
Neutron 0 Nucleus
Electron -1 Outside nucleus
Atomic Number: Number of protons in an atom.
Mass Number: Number of protons + number of neutrons.

3. Periodic Table & Periodicity

The periodic table arranges elements according to their atomic numbers and recurring chemical properties.

Important Trends

  • Atomic radius
  • Ionization energy
  • Electron affinity
  • Electronegativity
  • Metallic character
MDCAT tip: Learn the periodic trends and their reasons, not just the direction of arrows.

4. Chemical Bonding

Chemical bonding is the force that holds atoms together.

Main Types

  • Ionic bond
  • Covalent bond
  • Coordinate covalent bond
  • Hydrogen bonding
  • Metallic bonding
Ionic bond: Formed through transfer of electrons between atoms.
Covalent bond: Formed through sharing of electrons.

5. States of Matter

State Shape Volume
Solid Fixed Fixed
Liquid Not fixed Fixed
Gas Not fixed Not fixed

6. Solutions

A solution is a homogeneous mixture of solute and solvent.

Molarity = Moles of solute ÷ Volume of solution in litres

Solute: substance that is dissolved.

Solvent: substance that dissolves the solute.

7. Chemical Kinetics

Chemical kinetics is the study of the rate of chemical reactions.

Factors Affecting Reaction Rate

  • Temperature
  • Concentration
  • Surface area
  • Catalyst
  • Nature of reactants

8. Chemical Equilibrium

Chemical equilibrium occurs when the forward and reverse reactions occur at equal rates.

At equilibrium, the concentrations of reactants and products remain constant, but the reactions continue.

9. Acids, Bases & Salts

Acid: A substance that can donate H⁺ ions.
Base: A substance that can accept H⁺ ions or produce OH⁻ ions in aqueous solution.
pH = -log[H⁺]

pH less than 7 → acidic
pH = 7 → neutral
pH greater than 7 → basic

10. Electrochemistry

Electrochemistry deals with the relationship between chemical reactions and electrical energy.

  • Oxidation
  • Reduction
  • Redox reactions
  • Electrochemical cells
OIL: Oxidation Is Loss of electrons.
RIG: Reduction Is Gain of electrons.

11. Thermochemistry

Thermochemistry is the study of heat changes during chemical reactions.

Exothermic reaction: Releases heat.
Endothermic reaction: Absorbs heat.

12. Organic Chemistry

Organic chemistry is mainly the study of carbon-containing compounds.

Important Areas

  • Carbon bonding
  • Structural formulas
  • Isomerism
  • Functional groups
  • Organic reactions

13. Hydrocarbons

📝 MDCAT Chemistry Practice MCQs

1. One mole contains:

A) 6.022 × 10²³ particles

B) 3.011 × 10²³ particles

C) 9.8 × 10²³ particles

D) 1.66 × 10⁻²⁴ particles

Answer: A

2. The charge on an electron is:

A) +1

B) 0

C) -1

D) +2

Answer: C

3. Atomic number represents the number of:

A) Neutrons

B) Protons

C) Electrons + neutrons

D) Nucleons

Answer: B

4. Which bond involves sharing of electrons?

A) Ionic

B) Covalent

C) Metallic

D) Hydrogen

Answer: B

5. A solution contains:

A) Only solute

B) Only solvent

C) Solute and solvent

D) Only water

Answer: C

6. A catalyst generally:

A) Increases activation energy

B) Decreases activation energy

C) Stops the reaction

D) Is always consumed

Answer: B

7. A solution with pH 3 is:

A) Acidic

B) Basic

C) Neutral

D) Salt

Answer: A

8. Oxidation involves:

A) Gain of electrons

B) Loss of electrons

C) Gain of neutrons

D) Loss of protons

Answer: B

9. General formula of alkanes is:

A) CₙH₂ₙ

B) CₙH₂ₙ₋₂

C) CₙH₂ₙ₊₂

D) CₙHₙ

Answer: C

10. Which functional group is present in alcohols?

A) -COOH

B) -OH

C) -CHO

D) -NH₂

Answer: B

11. An exothermic reaction:

A) Absorbs heat

B) Releases heat

C) Produces no energy

D) Always requires light

Answer: B

12. The limiting reactant is the reactant that:

A) Remains in excess

B) Is consumed first

C) Is never used

D) Acts as a catalyst

Answer: B

13. The nucleus contains:

A) Electrons only

B) Protons and neutrons

C) Electrons and neutrons

D) Electrons and protons

Answer: B

14. Which is a hydrocarbon?

A) CH₄

B) H₂O

C) NH₃

D) NaCl

Answer: A

15. The pH of a neutral solution at 25°C is:

A) 0

B) 5

C) 7

D) 14

Answer: C

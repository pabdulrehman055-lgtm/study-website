📘 Chapter 1: Number Systems

Real Numbers

Real numbers include rational and irrational numbers. Examples are 2, 5/3, √2 and π.

Rational Numbers

A rational number can be written in the form p/q, where p and q are integers and q is not zero.

Irrational Numbers

Irrational numbers cannot be written in the form p/q. Examples include √2 and √3.

📗 Chapter 2: Algebra

Algebraic Expressions

An algebraic expression contains numbers, variables and mathematical operations.

Linear Equations

A linear equation is an equation in which the highest power of the variable is 1.

📝 Chapter 1 Practice

📝 Chapter 2 Practice

1. Solve: x + 5 = 12
2. Solve: 2x = 18
3. Simplify: 3x + 2x

✅ Answers

Chapter 1:

1. √2 is an irrational number.

2. 3/5 = 0.6

3. Yes, 7 is a rational number.

Chapter 2:

1. x + 5 = 12 → x = 7

2. 2x = 18 → x = 9

3. 3x + 2x = 5x

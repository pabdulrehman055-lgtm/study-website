Chapter 16: Plant Growth & Hormones

Plant growth is a permanent increase in size, mass or cell number. Plants continue to grow throughout much of their life because specialized regions contain actively dividing cells.

MDCAT Focus: Learn meristems, growth regions, plant hormones, phototropism, geotropism and the effects of auxin, gibberellins, cytokinins, abscisic acid and ethylene.

1. Plant Growth

Plant growth involves cell division, cell enlargement and differentiation.

Main Stages

  • Cell division: increases the number of cells.
  • Cell enlargement: cells increase in size.
  • Cell differentiation: cells become specialized.

2. Meristematic Tissue

Meristems contain actively dividing plant cells. They are responsible for producing new cells that contribute to plant growth.

Apical Meristems

Apical meristems are found near the tips of roots and shoots. They are mainly responsible for increasing the length of the plant.

Lateral Meristems

Lateral meristems contribute to an increase in the thickness or girth of stems and roots.

Remember:
Apical meristem → length growth
Lateral meristem → thickness/girth growth

3. Plant Growth Regulators

Plant hormones are chemical substances produced in small amounts that regulate growth and development.

Important plant hormones include:

  • Auxins
  • Gibberellins
  • Cytokinins
  • Abscisic acid (ABA)
  • Ethylene

4. Auxins

Auxins are important plant growth regulators involved in cell elongation and several developmental responses.

Important Functions

  • Promote cell elongation in many plant tissues.
  • Help maintain apical dominance.
  • Participate in root initiation at suitable concentrations.
  • Play an important role in tropic responses.
Exam Point: Auxin is strongly associated with cell elongation and phototropism.

5. Gibberellins

Gibberellins are plant hormones that influence growth and development.

  • Promote stem elongation in many plants.
  • Help break dormancy in some seeds.
  • Can promote seed germination.
  • May influence flowering and fruit development.

6. Cytokinins

Cytokinins promote cell division and influence plant development.

  • Promote cell division.
  • Support development of shoots.
  • Can delay leaf senescence under suitable conditions.
Remember: Cytokinin → strongly associated with cell division.

7. Abscisic Acid (ABA)

Abscisic acid is an important plant hormone involved in stress responses and growth regulation.

  • Promotes stomatal closure during water stress.
  • Helps maintain seed dormancy.
  • Can inhibit growth under certain conditions.
Exam Point: ABA is strongly associated with stress responses, stomatal closure and dormancy.

8. Ethylene

Ethylene is a gaseous plant hormone involved in several aspects of plant development.

  • Promotes fruit ripening.
  • Participates in leaf and fruit abscission.
  • Influences responses to environmental stress.
Remember: Ethylene → especially important in fruit ripening.

9. Phototropism

Phototropism is the directional growth of a plant part in response to light.

Positive Phototropism

A shoot growing toward light shows positive phototropism.

Role of Auxin

In shoots, auxin redistribution can cause greater cell elongation on the shaded side, causing the shoot to bend toward light.

Example: Shoot → usually positive phototropism.

10. Gravitropism

Gravitropism, also called geotropism, is directional growth in response to gravity.

  • Roots generally show positive gravitropism.
  • Shoots generally show negative gravitropism.
Easy Trick:
Root → grows with gravity → positive gravitropism
Shoot → grows against gravity → negative gravitropism

11. Seed Dormancy & Germination

Seed dormancy is a period during which a viable seed does not germinate even when some environmental conditions may appear suitable.

Germination begins when suitable conditions are present, commonly including adequate water, oxygen and an appropriate temperature.

Role of Hormones

  • Gibberellins can promote processes associated with germination.
  • ABA is associated with maintaining dormancy.

12. Apical Dominance

Apical dominance is the suppression of growth of lateral buds by the shoot apex.

Auxin produced in the shoot apex has an important role in this process.

13. Quick Hormone Comparison

  • Auxin: cell elongation, tropic responses, apical dominance.
  • Gibberellins: stem elongation, germination and development.
  • Cytokinins: cell division and shoot development.
  • ABA: dormancy, stress response and stomatal closure.
  • Ethylene: fruit ripening and abscission.

Quick Revision

  • Meristems contain actively dividing cells.
  • Apical meristems increase plant length.
  • Lateral meristems increase thickness/girth.
  • Auxin is important for cell elongation.
  • Auxin plays an important role in phototropism.
  • Gibberellins can promote stem elongation and germination.
  • Cytokinins promote cell division.
  • ABA is associated with dormancy and stress responses.
  • Ethylene promotes fruit ripening.
  • Shoots usually show positive phototropism.
  • Roots usually show positive gravitropism.

MDCAT-Style MCQs

1. Which tissue contains actively dividing cells?

A) Meristematic tissue

B) Xylem

C) Sclerenchyma

D) Cork

Answer: A) Meristematic tissue

2. Apical meristem mainly increases:

A) Plant length

B) Fruit sweetness

C) Leaf color

D) Seed number

Answer: A) Plant length

3. Which hormone is strongly associated with cell elongation?

A) Auxin

B) ABA

C) Ethylene

D) Cytokinin

Answer: A) Auxin

4. Which hormone promotes cell division?

A) Cytokinin

B) Ethylene

C) ABA

D) Auxin only

Answer: A) Cytokinin

5. Which hormone is associated with fruit ripening?

A) Ethylene

B) Cytokinin

C) ABA

D) Auxin

Answer: A) Ethylene

6. ABA is strongly associated with:

A) Seed dormancy

B) Fruit ripening only

C) Cell wall digestion

D) DNA replication

Answer: A) Seed dormancy

7. Growth of a shoot toward light is called:

A) Positive phototropism

B) Negative phototropism

C) Positive hydrotropism

D) Negative gravitropism only

Answer: A) Positive phototropism

8. Roots generally show:

A) Positive gravitropism

B) Negative gravitropism

C) No response to gravity

D) Positive phototropism

Answer: A) Positive gravitropism

9. Which hormone can promote stem elongation?

A) Gibberellin

B) ABA only

C) Ethylene only

D) Cytokinin only

Answer: A) Gibberellin

10. Which hormone helps plants respond to water stress by promoting stomatal closure?

A) ABA

B) Ethylene

C) Gibberellin

D) Cytokinin

Answer: A) ABA

11. Lateral meristems mainly contribute to:

A) Increase in girth

B) Fruit ripening

C) Photosynthesis only

D) Seed dormancy only

Answer: A) Increase in girth

12. Which hormone is associated with apical dominance?

A) Auxin

B) Ethylene

C) ABA

D) Gibberellin only

Answer: A) Auxin

13. Which hormone is associated with maintaining seed dormancy?

A) ABA

B) Ethylene

C) Cytokinin

D) Auxin

Answer: A) ABA

14. Which hormone is a gas?

A) Ethylene

B) Auxin

C) Cytokinin

D) ABA

Answer: A) Ethylene

15. Which process includes cell division, cell enlargement and differentiation?

A) Plant growth

B) Transpiration only

C) Respiration only

D) Digestion

Answer: A) Plant growth

Exam Tips 🎯

  • Memorize the major functions of the five important plant hormones.
  • Auxin → elongation and tropic responses.
  • Gibberellin → stem elongation and germination.
  • Cytokinin → cell division.
  • ABA → dormancy and stress response.
  • Ethylene → fruit ripening.
  • Apical → length; lateral → thickness.
  • Root → positive gravitropism.
  • Shoot → positive phototropism.

Chapter 1: Physical Quantities & Measurements

Physical Quantities

A physical quantity is a property that can be measured and expressed using a numerical value and a unit.

SI Base Quantities

  • Length: metre (m)
  • Mass: kilogram (kg)
  • Time: second (s)
  • Electric current: ampere (A)
  • Temperature: kelvin (K)
  • Amount of substance: mole (mol)
  • Luminous intensity: candela (cd)

Derived Quantities

Derived quantities are obtained from combinations of base quantities.

  • Speed: metre per second (m/s)
  • Acceleration: metre per second squared (m/s²)
  • Force: newton (N)
  • Energy: joule (J)

Important Formula

Speed = Distance ÷ Time

📝 Practice MCQs

1. What is the SI unit of length?

A) Kilogram

B) Metre

C) Second

D) Ampere

Answer: B) Metre

2. What is the SI unit of mass?

A) Gram

B) Newton

C) Kilogram

D) Joule

Answer: C) Kilogram

3. What is the SI unit of time?

A) Minute

B) Hour

C) Second

D) Day

Answer: C) Second

4. Which of the following is a derived quantity?

A) Length

B) Mass

C) Time

D) Speed

Answer: D) Speed

5. Speed is calculated by:

A) Time ÷ Distance

B) Distance ÷ Time

C) Distance × Time

D) Distance + Time

Answer: B) Distance ÷ Time

🎯 Quick Revision

  • Length → metre (m)
  • Mass → kilogram (kg)
  • Time → second (s)
  • Electric current → ampere (A)
  • Speed → distance ÷ time
  • Force → newton (N)
  • Energy → joule (J)

🧲 1. What is Electromagnetism?

Electromagnetism is the branch of physics that studies the relationship between electricity and magnetism.

An electric current can produce a magnetic field, and magnetic effects can be used to produce electrical effects.

🧲 2. Magnetic Field

A magnetic field is the region around a magnet or a current-carrying conductor where magnetic forces can be experienced.

The magnetic field is represented by magnetic field lines.

  • Magnetic field lines form closed loops.
  • They are closer together where the field is stronger.
  • Magnetic field lines do not cross each other.

⚡ 3. Magnetic Field Due to Current

When electric current flows through a conductor, it produces a magnetic field around the conductor.

The direction of the magnetic field around a straight current- carrying conductor can be determined using the right-hand rule.

✋ 4. Right-Hand Rule

According to the right-hand rule, if a straight conductor is held in the right hand with the thumb pointing in the direction of conventional current, the curled fingers show the direction of the magnetic field.

🧲 5. Electromagnet

An electromagnet is a temporary magnet produced by passing electric current through a coil of wire.

A soft iron core is often placed inside the coil to increase the strength of the magnetic field.

Electromagnets are used in devices such as electric bells, relays and lifting machines.

🔄 6. Force on a Current-Carrying Conductor

A current-carrying conductor placed in a magnetic field can experience a magnetic force.

F = BIL sin θ

Where:

  • F = magnetic force
  • B = magnetic field strength
  • I = current
  • L = length of conductor
  • θ = angle between conductor and magnetic field

⚙️ 7. Electric Motor

An electric motor is a device that converts electrical energy into mechanical energy.

It works on the principle that a current-carrying conductor placed in a magnetic field experiences a force.

🧭 8. Earth's Magnetic Field

The Earth behaves approximately like a giant magnet and has a magnetic field around it.

A compass works because its magnetic needle aligns itself approximately with Earth's magnetic field.

📝 Practice MCQs

1. Electromagnetism deals with the relationship between:

A) Heat and sound

B) Electricity and magnetism

C) Mass and volume

D) Light and temperature

Answer: B) Electricity and magnetism

2. A current-carrying conductor produces:

A) Only heat

B) A magnetic field

C) No effect

D) Gravity

Answer: B) A magnetic field

3. Which rule determines the direction of the magnetic field around a straight current-carrying conductor?

A) Left-hand rule

B) Right-hand rule

C) Hooke's law

D) Ohm's law

Answer: B) Right-hand rule

4. An electric motor converts:

A) Mechanical energy into electrical energy

B) Electrical energy into mechanical energy

C) Heat into mass

D) Sound into electricity

Answer: B) Electrical energy into mechanical energy

5. A common material used as the core of an electromagnet is:

A) Glass

B) Wood

C) Soft iron

D) Plastic

Answer: C) Soft iron

⚡ Quick Revision

  • Electromagnetism connects electricity and magnetism.
  • A current-carrying conductor produces a magnetic field.
  • The right-hand rule gives the direction of the magnetic field.
  • An electromagnet is a temporary magnet produced by current.
  • A current-carrying conductor can experience magnetic force.
  • Magnetic force: F = BIL sin θ.
  • An electric motor converts electrical energy into mechanical energy.
  • Earth has its own magnetic field.

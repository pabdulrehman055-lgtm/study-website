📘 1. What is Thermodynamics?

Thermodynamics is the branch of physics that deals with heat, temperature, energy and their transformations.

It explains how thermal energy can be transferred from one system to another and how energy changes during physical processes.

🌡️ 2. Temperature and Heat

Temperature is a measure of the degree of hotness or coldness of a body.

Heat is energy transferred from a hotter body to a colder body because of a temperature difference.

Heat naturally flows from a body at higher temperature to a body at lower temperature.

⚖️ 3. Thermal Equilibrium

When two bodies are in contact and reach the same temperature, they are said to be in thermal equilibrium.

At thermal equilibrium, there is no net transfer of heat between the two bodies.

🔥 4. Internal Energy

Internal energy is the total microscopic energy present inside a system.

It includes the kinetic and potential energies associated with the particles of the system.

📐 5. First Law of Thermodynamics

The first law of thermodynamics is based on the law of conservation of energy.

Energy cannot be created or destroyed; it can only be transferred or transformed.

Q = ΔU + W

Where:

  • Q = heat supplied to the system
  • ΔU = change in internal energy
  • W = work done by the system

🌡️ 6. Heat Capacity

Heat capacity is the amount of heat required to raise the temperature of a body by one degree.

Specific heat capacity is the amount of heat required to raise the temperature of one unit mass of a substance by one degree.

🔥 7. Modes of Heat Transfer

1. Conduction

Conduction is the transfer of heat through a substance without the bulk movement of the substance.

2. Convection

Convection is the transfer of heat through the actual movement of a fluid such as a liquid or gas.

3. Radiation

Radiation is the transfer of heat through electromagnetic waves. It does not require a material medium.

♻️ 8. Second Law of Thermodynamics

The second law of thermodynamics describes the natural direction of heat transfer and the limitations of converting heat completely into useful work.

Heat naturally flows from a hotter object toward a colder object.

⚙️ 9. Heat Engine

A heat engine is a device that converts part of the heat energy supplied to it into mechanical work.

A heat engine generally operates between a hot reservoir and a cold reservoir.

📝 Practice MCQs

1. Thermodynamics deals mainly with:

A) Light only

B) Heat and energy

C) Electricity only

D) Sound only

Answer: B) Heat and energy

2. Heat naturally flows from:

A) Cold body to hot body

B) Hot body to cold body

C) Low pressure to high pressure only

D) Cold body to cold body

Answer: B) Hot body to cold body

3. Which equation represents the first law of thermodynamics?

A) F = ma

B) v = fλ

C) Q = ΔU + W

D) P = F/A

Answer: C) Q = ΔU + W

4. Heat transfer through electromagnetic waves is called:

A) Conduction

B) Convection

C) Radiation

D) Equilibrium

Answer: C) Radiation

5. At thermal equilibrium, two bodies have:

A) Different temperatures

B) The same temperature

C) Zero mass

D) Infinite energy

Answer: B) The same temperature

⚡ Quick Revision

  • Thermodynamics deals with heat, temperature and energy.
  • Heat flows naturally from hot to cold.
  • Equal temperature means thermal equilibrium.
  • Internal energy is the microscopic energy of a system.
  • First law: Q = ΔU + W.
  • Heat transfer occurs by conduction, convection and radiation.
  • A heat engine converts part of heat energy into useful work.
